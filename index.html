Plan for PIQ

Where PIQ is going.

01Where we are

PersistIQ has grown to where running the operation and driving the strategy are two different jobs. I'm splitting them so my time goes to what grows the accounts: pilots, reply-rate, retention strategy, instead of day-to-day firefighting.

02The restructure, in motion this week

Already happening, no approval needed. Estefania takes on some of the KOCs, becomes first filter for the AMs, and is first responder on retention. Her blue-flagged accounts move to María: Sustainable Materials, Voicenx, Clear Plan, Atica Global, Mimosa Life Sciences.

03Channel strategy

Month 1: Outbound Essentials — fully managed cold email. Month 2: SEO runs in parallel as a retention tool. When cold email hits its ceiling: LinkedIn Outreach via HeyReach at $200–250/sender/mo, SEO at $850/mo, LinkedIn Ads at $350/mo + client budget.

04The add-on package

Package add-ons on top of Outbound Essentials and write them into SOWs as a replacement for termination. Outsource to start, build in-house once volume justifies it. Lock down dedicated Wishpond resource with Mike before Ads launch.

05The bigger play

Full B2B outreach chain on the same ICP: cold email + LinkedIn Outreach + SEO + LinkedIn Ads. Each channel outsourced first, then brought in-house once margin and delivery are ours.

★How we'll run priorities

New work comes in by replacing, not adding.

When something new comes up, we open the board together and decide what moves down.
