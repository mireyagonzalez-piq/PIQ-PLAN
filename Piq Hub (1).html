Plan for PIQ

Where PIQ is going.

01Where we are

PersistIQ has grown to where running the operation and driving the strategy are two different jobs. I'm splitting them so my time goes to what grows the accounts: pilots, reply-rate, retention strategy, instead of day-to-day firefighting.

02The restructure, in motion this week

Already happening, no approval needed. This is just visibility: Estefania takes on some of the KOCs (not all), becomes first filter for the AMs (Roberto, Eduardo, Lorena, Tania, María), and is first responder on retention, bringing me a recommendation, not just a problem.

Her blue-flagged accounts move to María: Sustainable Materials, Voicenx, Clear Plan, Atica Global, Mimosa Life Sciences. That's what frees her time.

03Channel strategy: when cold email hits its ceiling

Month 1: Outbound Essentials is the base, fully managed cold email, every client starts here.

Month 2: SEO runs in parallel as a retention tool, giving clients a reason to stay even before the inbox converts. It's not just acquisition, it's proof of value while cold email warms up.

When cold email hits its ceiling for a segment, we don't wait for the client to churn, we add or swap a channel:

LinkedIn Outreach (1-to-1 DMs via HeyReach) is the easy add. Sell at $200–250/sender/mo (cost ~$105), no new approval needed, integrates with PersistIQ via webhook, fits the current structure. SEO at $850/mo basics. Can be sold as a paid add-on for new revenue, or bundled in as a retention tool to save an at-risk account. LinkedIn Ads (paid media, separate from Outreach) at $350/mo evergreen + the client's ad budget (min $50/day), but it's the harder one: PIQ can't run it in-house yet and needs a dedicated Wishpond resource. Ad-hoc bandwidth has failed before. Dedicated is the only model that works.

Industry fit decides the mix per client; there's no single rule. For some verticals LinkedIn is the stronger channel, for others cold email or SEO carries more weight. We pick the combination that fits each client's ICP rather than defaulting to one play for everyone.

04The add-on package, needs your approval

Package the add-ons on top of Outbound Essentials (LinkedIn Outreach, SEO, LinkedIn Ads) and write them into SOWs as a replacement for termination when cold email underperforms. Instead of losing the client, you swap or add the channel. This is how retention stops being a firefight and starts being a structured offer.

Delivery model: outsource to start, build in-house once volume justifies it. The specific thing to lock down with Mike: dedicated Wishpond resource for PIQ, or ad-hoc? That answer determines whether Ads can actually launch.

05The bigger play: PIQ as a B2B outreach chain

Today we're cold email. The direction is a full B2B outreach chain on the same ICP: cold email + LinkedIn Outreach (1-to-1 DMs) + SEO + LinkedIn Ads (paid media). Same PersistIQ logic, more channels, more reasons for a client to stay and fewer reasons to leave.

Each new channel gets outsourced first to prove it, then brought in-house once the margin and the delivery are ours.
