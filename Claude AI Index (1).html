Where PIQ is going.

01Where we are

PersistIQ has grown to where running the operation and driving the strategy are two different jobs. I'm splitting them so my time goes to what grows the accounts: pilots, reply-rate, retention strategy, instead of day-to-day firefighting.

02The restructure, in motion this week

Already happening, no approval needed. This is just visibility: Estefania takes the KOCs, becomes first filter for the AMs (Roberto, Eduardo, Lorena, Tania, María), and is first responder on retention, bringing me a recommendation, not just a problem.

Her blue-flagged accounts move to María: Sustainable Materials, Voicenx, Clear Plan, Atica Global, Mimosa Life Sciences. That's what frees her time.

03Channel strategy: when cold email hits its ceiling

Month 1: SEO runs in parallel as a retention tool, giving clients a reason to stay even before the inbox converts. It's not just acquisition, it's proof of value while cold email warms up.

Month 2+: if cold email isn't producing for a segment, we don't wait for the client to churn. We pivot the channel or run a second one alongside it. Options: HeyReach (LinkedIn outreach) at $79/sender/mo, already integrates with Instantly via API and webhooks, fits inside the current PIQ structure with no major overhaul. LinkedIn Ads is the harder one: PIQ can't execute it in-house right now, so it needs a dedicated Wishpond resource assigned to PIQ specifically. Ad-hoc bandwidth has failed before. Dedicated is the only model that works.

Industry fit: for verticals where LinkedIn is the right channel, we lead with HeyReach or Ads. For everyone else, cold email + SEO is the play.

04The add-on package, needs your approval

Bundle SEO + HeyReach + LinkedIn Ads as an add-on, and write it into SOWs as a replacement for termination when cold email underperforms. Instead of losing the client, you swap or add the channel. This is how retention stops being a firefight and starts being a structured offer.

Delivery model: outsource to start, build in-house once volume justifies it. The specific thing to lock down with Mike: dedicated Wishpond resource for PIQ, or ad-hoc? That answer determines whether Ads can actually launch.

05The bigger play: PIQ as a B2B outreach chain

Today we're cold email. The direction is a full B2B outreach chain on the same ICP: email + LinkedIn outreach (HeyReach) + LinkedIn Ads. Same PersistIQ logic, more channels, more reasons for a client to stay and fewer reasons to leave.

Each new channel gets outsourced first to prove it, then brought in-house once the margin and the delivery are ours.
